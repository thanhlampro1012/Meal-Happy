Be Vietnam Pro
==============

Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bevietnampro
Project: https://github.com/bettergui/BeVietnamPro
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt)

The Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), and Bold (700) fonts
are self-hosted here. The original upstream TTF files were converted to
WOFF2 without subsetting or changing their outlines, character maps,
layout features, or naming. Every weight retains its full 459-character
Unicode map, including Vietnamese precomposed vowels and combining marks.

No third-party font request is required when the app runs.
